Founded in Experience

We've been where you are.

My journey started in a place I never imagined — behind bars. As a first-time offender, I spent years navigating a complex and often unforgiving legal system, hiring five different attorneys, and ultimately fighting for my own freedom.

I witnessed countless people in the law library fighting hopeless battles, limited by education, resources, and the daunting nature of the process itself. Many gave up. This experience became the foundation of Pro Se It Ain't So.

I was fortunate to have worked as an executive legal assistant and had always taken an interest in the law. But for the average incarcerated individual, the challenges are staggering. No one should give up simply because they lack access to professional document services.

— Makeda Davis, Founder

"The goal is for documents from self-represented individuals to look professional and well-organized — hence the name Pro Se It Ain't So." Makeda Davis  ·  Founder, Speaker, Advocate

What We Do

Document services that speak for you.

We type, format, and organize your documents so they look professional and court-ready. You provide the content and direction — we handle the presentation.

📋 Habeas Corpus Petition Typing

Professional typing and formatting of habeas corpus petitions based on your exact written specifications.

⚖️ Post-Conviction Motion Formatting

Typing and formatting for 440 motions, 2255 motions, and other post-conviction documents you specify.

📑 Appeals Document Typing

Professional typing for notices of appeal, briefs, and related documents using your content and instructions.

🎯 Parole & Clemency Documents

Formatting and typing services for parole packets, clemency applications, and related materials you author.

🔍 Case Law Location Services

Locating publicly available case law, statutes, and court rules. Factual information only — not legal interpretation.

💬 Family Coordination Support

Communication assistance to help family members understand procedural information and coordinate document submission.
